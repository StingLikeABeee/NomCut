Choose your installation

One-click installer for Windows, plus streamlined options for macOS, Linux, and AI coding agents.

⬇ Download NomCut-Setup.bat
  1. Double-click the downloaded file. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway (the script is plain text and can be inspected in Notepad).
  2. It verifies your environment and installs Python 3.12 and FFmpeg automatically without requiring admin privileges.
  3. Choose whether to enable optional GPU acceleration and local Ollama AI titles, then NomCut Studio opens in your browser.
  4. Launch anytime from the NomCut shortcut on your Desktop.
Everything installs cleanly inside %LOCALAPPDATA%\NomCut. Delete that folder anytime to uninstall.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NomCut completely free?

Yes. NomCut is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no hidden tiers, and no watermarks.

Are my videos uploaded to any server?

Never. All downloading, speech recognition, highlight scoring, and video rendering happen locally on your own computer.

What hardware do I need?

A 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC (or macOS/Linux machine) with at least 6 GB of free disk space. An NVIDIA GPU speeds up encoding and local Whisper transcription, but CPU mode works out of the box.

Can I use NomCut from my phone?

Yes! When you launch NomCut using the NomCut (phone access) shortcut, you can connect your phone over your home Wi-Fi using a 6-digit PIN to monitor live streams, trigger instant cuts, and download clips.

How do I uninstall NomCut?

Simply delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\NomCut folder and remove the Desktop shortcuts.
